# Breakdown to video-edit: a style target for our own footage

Use it when the aim is "cut my clips the way this film is cut", not to copy the film.

- `video/rhythm.md`: cuts per minute, average shot length, the change curve (one digit per 0.5 s), times of
  cuts and transitions with source frame numbers. Turn these into a pacing target for the cut script
  (video-edit step 3), for example "4-5 s average, a jump cut inside long takes".
- `video/look.md`: shot size mix, fixed vs moving camera, grade description. Use as a checklist when
  choosing cutaways; do not grade to match by numbers, compare by eye on a contact sheet.
- `video/sound.md` and `stems/`: whether the music is a bed laid across cuts or per shot, voice share of
  the duration, effect anchors. Own score and own licensed or generated music only; the original's
  `music.wav` is for listening, never for the master.
- `video/packaging.md` and `packaging/overlays.ass`: subtitle timing and style as a reference for burned
  captions.
- Do the checks video-edit already requires on the result: read the cut as text, sheet of cutaways, critic.
  A breakdown of the target is not a replacement for looking at our own cut.
