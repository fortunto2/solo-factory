# 07 · Pitch

- **When:** the last hours before submission, and the minutes before the demo.
- **Inputs:** `docs/event.md` (judging criteria, demo length, video limit), the working demo,
  `docs/gallery.md` or `docs/assessment.md` if the field is known.
- **Output files:** `docs/pitch.md` (script + cheat sheet), the deck, the platform text.

Other skills do the heavy parts: `/supervox` pitch coach mode for the Q&A cheat sheet and live
prompts during questions, `/video-promo` for a demo video script, `/humanize` on the submission
text before it goes out.

## Shape

1. The person and the moment it hurts, in one sentence. Not "I have many ideas".
2. Who pays, and why now.
3. The demo: the one path that works, live if the venue network allows, recorded if not.
4. One number you measured, with its setup. Stage numbers you did not measure are not said.
5. What is not validated yet, said before a judge asks.

Map each judging criterion from `event.md` to a slide or a demo moment; a criterion with no
moment is a point given away.

## Q&A cheat sheet (bottom of `docs/pitch.md`)

The five hardest questions with a one-line answer each, starting with the ones the field invites
("how is this different from <strongest neighbour>?", "who pays?", "what did you measure?").
In `/supervox` pitch coach mode the answer to the question just asked goes to the top of the panel.

## Don't

- Don't claim what the product does not do; say what it does instead ("a wind cone, not a
  dispersion model").
- Don't add a new concept on the last morning; it takes over the Q&A.
