# recap (default)

Use when the type is unclear. Short, neutral, grounded.

- **Header**: date, duration, who. Add "type unclear, used recap" if that is why you are here.
- **Gist**: 2-3 sentences. What was this call about and what came out of it.
- **Decisions**: what was agreed, with the quote and time.
- **Tasks**: owner, verb, object; date only if spoken.
- **Open questions**: asked and not answered, or "we should" with no owner.
- **Numbers and names**: amounts, dates, products, people, each with a timestamp; flag ASR doubt.
- **Insights**: only ideas the owner reacted to or noted; skip generic observations.
