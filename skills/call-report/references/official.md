# official (government, immigration, grant, bank, tax, legal)

Exactness matters more than readability. Private, never summarized elsewhere.

- **Header**: institution, department or person, date, channel (phone, visit), language used.
- **Requirements, verbatim**: each as quoted with `t`; no paraphrase of rules, thresholds or legal terms.
- **Documents asked**: list, with form, copies, translation or notarization if said.
- **Deadlines**: date and what it applies to; mark ones the owner must infer.
- **Reference numbers**: case, application, ticket, phone, email, names of officials; read digits back as heard and flag every uncertain one with `[ASR gap?]`.
- **Fees and amounts**: figure, currency, where to pay.
- **Contradictions**: anything that differs from what the owner expected or was told before.
- **Next step**: who acts, what, by when.
