# public-talk (panel, talk, meetup, demo day, hackathon, podcast)

Public by nature: paraphrase is fine ("a panelist said"), names as spoken.

- **Header**: event, date, speakers (confirm names against the event page, not the ASR).
- **Short version**: 3 lines: what it was, the strongest claim, why it matters to the owner.
- **Who said what**: one block per speaker, their 2-4 sharpest points, each with a quote and `t`.
- **Ideas to steal**: concrete, with the line that triggered each and how it maps to the owner's work.
- **People to approach**: who, why, what to say; mark guesses from ASR names.
- **Claims to verify**: numbers or statements worth checking against a public source.
- **Demo scorecard** (demo day, hackathon): one row per team: name as heard, idea in a line, judges' questions, what was claimed on stage, a score or a one-word verdict.
- **Questions asked**: audience and judge questions, with answers given.
