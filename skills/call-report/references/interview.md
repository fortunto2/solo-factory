# interview (customer or user interview)

- **Header**: who (role, company type), how they were found, date.
- **Problems**: each in their words, quoted, with how often and how painful they said it is.
- **Current alternatives**: what they use today, what it costs them, why they stay.
- **Quotes worth keeping**: 3-6 lines, verbatim, with `t`; no editing for polish.
- **Hypotheses**: for each hypothesis the owner held (take them from notes or the start question): confirmed, killed or untested, with the quote that decided it.
- **Surprises**: what the owner did not expect.
- **Would they pay or act**: any real signal (asked for price, offered intro, asked to be told when ready) versus polite interest; label which.
- **Follow-ups**: who to ask next, what to test.
