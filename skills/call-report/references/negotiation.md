# negotiation (pressure, boundaries, deal terms, `negotiation` role)

Grounded in the transcript. Private area when the call was personal.

- **Header**: who, date, duration, the owner's stated limits / walk-away point if he gave them (marked `(from owner)`).
- **Timeline**: asks and offers in order, `12:40 Anna asks X; 15:05 owner offers Y`. Numbers and dates carry `[ASR gap?]` when unsure.
- **Concessions**: what each side gave up and what they got back.
- **Tactics observed**: each as "possible <tactic>", with the quote, timestamp and speaker. Include only clear cases; honest hard bargaining is not a tactic. Tactic names come from `coach-roles.md`.
- **Boundaries**: held (what the owner declined or reserved) and crossed (what he accepted past his stated limits), each with a quote.
- **Agreed / not agreed**: the state at the end, in the words used.
- **Next move**: one line: what the owner should do or answer next, and by when if a date was spoken.

Never advise manipulating back. Next moves are honest asks, questions or a stated limit.
