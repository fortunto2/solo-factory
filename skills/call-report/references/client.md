# client (sales, partner, vendor, consulting)

Private: route to the project, not to a public place.

- **Context**: who they are, why this call, where the relationship stands.
- **Stakeholders**: name, role, stance (champion, blocker, unknown), each grounded in a line.
- **Pains**: their words, quoted, ranked by how much airtime or emotion they got.
- **BANT-lite**: budget, authority, need, timeline: one line each, `not discussed` if so. Never fill a blank.
- **Objections**: the objection, our answer, whether it landed.
- **Commitments**: what each side promised, owner, date only if spoken.
- **Money**: prices, discounts, terms, currency, exactly as said; flag ASR doubt on every number.
- **Next step**: one concrete action with owner and date, or `none agreed`.
- **Follow-up email**: draft in the owner's voice, short, quoting their own words back; only after he says yes.
