# discussion (dispute, difficult conversation, `mediator` or `psych` roles)

Neutral by default. Private area only when the call was personal (see routing in SKILL.md).

- **Header**: who, date, duration, role used.
- **Positions**: each person's stated position in one line with a quote and timestamp.
- **Needs**: what seems to lie under each position, marked "possible". Only what the words support.
- **Agreements**: what was settled, in the words used, with who agreed.
- **Open conflicts**: unresolved points, one line each, and what each side said about them.
- **What helped**: moments that lowered tension (a question, an apology, a pause), quoted.
- **What hurt**: moments that raised it (absolutes, interruptions, contempt), quoted. Describe the line, not the person's character.
- **Next step**: one concrete thing each person may do, only if one was spoken or clearly implied.

No diagnoses, no blame verdicts, no winner. If the `psych` role ran, emotion and need words stay "possible".
